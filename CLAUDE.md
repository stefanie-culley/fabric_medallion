# CLAUDE.md

## Working style
- Keep tasks small. Do one task, report, and stop.
- Keep replies short enough to read without scrolling: a few lines, no long lists or recaps.
- When decisions or tasks are pending, present **one at a time** and wait for the answer before the next.
- For a decision, give a recommendation plus at most 2–3 options in one or two lines each.
- After each decision is recorded, commit and push to `origin/main` without asking.

## Naming
- Names must be readable by anyone new to the project: plain English, no type codes (`nb_`, `pl_`), no abbreviations. See [standards/03-naming.md](standards/03-naming.md).

## Repo
- Team standard for medallion architecture in Microsoft Fabric. See [README.md](README.md).
- Open decisions are marked **DECIDE** in `standards/`; record each answer as an ADR in `adr/`.
